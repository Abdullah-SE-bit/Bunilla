# Owner walkthrough — about one minute

“This is a proposal for how Bunilla could introduce itself before opening. The first screen keeps your original logo, colors and typography, and makes the promise clear: coffee, fresh bakes and wood-fire pizza, coming to I-8.

As we scroll, each category gets its own moment. The original cup and bakery imagery carry the food story; pizza has an illustrated concept until final photography is available. The warm story section introduces the neighborhood feeling without inventing your history.

The gallery gives your launch artwork room to breathe. At the end, visitors can follow the real Instagram profile for updates or email you. Everything works on a phone, including the menu, and the site is ready to demonstrate locally.”

## Tangible benefits

- Immediate recognition of the business, neighborhood and launch state.
- Original visual identity expressed at an editorial quality level.
- Clear, usable launch actions instead of a dead signup button.
- Fast local images, self-hosted fonts and a restrained animation layer.
- Centralized text and settings make owner revisions straightforward.

## Request before official launch

Approve the design and proposed copy; confirm asset rights and whether published venue imagery reflects actual premises; provide final food/venue photography, correct business operator details, opening information and contact confirmation. Confirm hosting domain and policy details. A real opt-in waitlist needs a chosen backend, credentials, consent and retention arrangements before it can be added.

After approval: incorporate factual edits/assets, review privacy/legal text, authorize hosting, configure origin and preview/indexing settings, verify staging, then publish only with explicit authorization. This project has not been deployed or substituted for the existing site.
